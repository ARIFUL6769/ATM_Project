Privacy Policy

Welcome to the ATM System. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use this web application.

1. Information Collection

This application may store account information, such as name, email address, account number, and PIN, to demonstrate basic ATM operations.

2. Use of Information

The information is used to support account login, balance checking, deposits, withdrawals, and money transfers within this application.

3. Data Security

Reasonable care should be taken to protect account information. This project is intended for learning purposes and is not a real banking service.

4. Information Sharing

Do not enter real banking details or sensitive personal information into this demonstration application.

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the project develops.
